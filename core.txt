1. IDEA (CURATED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ONE USER   : BMC (Bhopal Municipal Corporation) officer planning Durga visarjan.
ONE DATE   : Durga visarjan on 20 Oct 2026 (9 days after the event).
ONE OUTCOME: The officer opens one screen, sees which ghats are riskiest and
             WHY, can trace every number to the report page, and forwards a
             short Hindi + English brief.

THE CORE IDEA - "rank by WHY a ghat is risky, not just HOW risky"
Each ghat's risk is split into two parts and tied to different actions:
  - BASELINE problem : water already breaches CPCB class B limits BEFORE
                       immersion (pre phase). Needs lake/kund-level attention.
  - FESTIVAL effect  : water gets worse during/after immersion compared with
                       pre. Needs aeration / cleaning / testing on the day.
Every ghat also carries a CONFIDENCE rating, so a data hole never looks like
safety.

WHY THIS IS THE INSIGHT (descriptive pre-check on the organisers' CSV; small
samples; a pattern to explore, NOT a finding to publish):
  (Corrected 11 Oct 2026: comparisons below use MATCHED pairs only - the same
   festival-year table with valid pre and during readings. Earlier text
   compared averages taken over different tables.)
  - Bairagarh kund: DO in matched pairs 4.2 -> 2.2 (Durga 2024 p.25),
    5.6 -> 4.5 (Ganesh 2023 p.26), 5.2 -> 4.1 (Ganesh 2024 p.69), 0 -> 0
    (Durga 2023 p.60); paired means 3.75 -> 2.7 mg/l. Poor already, worse
    during. DO read 0 mg/l four times (2023 Ganesh after; 2023 Durga pre and
    during; 2025 Ganesh during, p.28 - the last has no pre reading).
  - Malikhedi kund: DO in matched pairs 4.6 -> 4.3 (Durga 2023 p.61),
    4.5 -> 4.1 (Durga 2024 p.26), 7.5 -> 7.0 (Ganesh 2023 p.27), 4.8 -> 3.8
    (Ganesh 2024 p.71); paired means 5.35 -> 4.8 mg/l, which crosses the
    5 mg/l limit. Separately, DO read 0.6 mg/l during Ganesh 2025 (Report
    2025 p.29) with NO pre reading in that table, so it cannot be paired; it
    is reported as an unpaired breach, not scored as a change.
  - Hathaikheda: DO stays within the limit (~7-8 mg/l) but BOD rises in
    matched pairs from a paired mean of 5.5 to 8.4 mg/l (all 6 tables
    paired). A different failure mode.
  - BOD breaches the 3 mg/l limit in ~80-100% of PRE readings at most ghats,
    so BOD alone would rank mostly by baseline. Hence the split.

APPENDIX A - SCORING SPEC (defaults; tunable in rules.yaml)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard: CPCB class B. Scored parameters: pH, Dissolved oxygen, BOD.
Other parameters (COD, solids, turbidity, conductivity, Cr, Pb, Zn, Cu) are
SHOWN as context with "no class B limit"; they are NOT scored. For metals,
show counts: quantified vs BDL vs not sampled.

Per-reading breach and exceedance (0..1):
  DO  : breach if v < 5   ; exceedance = clip((5 - v)/5, 0, 1)
  BOD : breach if v > 3   ; exceedance = clip((v - 3)/3, 0, 1)
  pH  : breach if v < 6.5 or v > 8.5
        exceedance = clip(distance_outside_range / 1.5, 0, 1)

Valid reading = status "value". Missing readings are excluded from rates and
counted against confidence. below_detection is not a breach.

BASELINE score (pre phase only), over all festival-years available:
  baseline = 0.5 * breach_rate + 0.5 * mean_exceedance   (mean over valid
  pre readings; non-breaches contribute 0 exceedance)

FESTIVAL score, per (festival, year, parameter) with valid pre and during:
  worsen(pre -> X):
     DO  : (pre - X) / 5       BOD : (X - pre) / 3
     pH  : (dist_out(X) - dist_out(pre)) / 1.5
  each clipped to 0..1 (improvement = 0)
  triple_score = 0.7 * worsen(pre->during) + 0.3 * worsen(pre->after)
  (if 'after' missing use during only; if 'during' missing skip the triple)
  festival_p = mean(triple_score) over that parameter's triples
  festival   = MAX over parameters of festival_p   (worst parameter decides;
               decided 11 Oct 2026, replaces mean over all triples; keep
               festival_p per parameter in the output for the detail view;
               rules.yaml scoring.festival_aggregation: max | mean_of_triples)

RISK = 100 * (0.5 * baseline + 0.5 * festival)       [w_base, w_fest in yaml]

CRITICAL FLAG: any reported DO == 0 mg/l -> tier floor = High and show
"reported 0 mg/l dissolved oxygen: <n> times (years/pages)". (Our own flag,
not a CPCB limit; wording must say so.)

TIER (calibrated in L2, 11 Oct 2026; placeholders were 60 / 35):
  High >= 27.5 | Medium 20-27.5 | Low < 20   (apply critical-flag floor)
  High 27.5 = 100*(0.5*0.40 + 0.5*0.15), derived from baseline cut-off 0.40
  and festival 0.15 (the festival driver cut-off until 11 Oct 2026; now kept
  only as rules.yaml festival_tier_anchor). Not re-tuned.
  Medium 20 = 100*(0.5*0.40), derived arithmetic; anchor choice is judgement.

DRIVER label (revised 11 Oct 2026):
  poor   = baseline >= 0.40
  worse  = PHYSICAL anchor on matched pairs (same table, valid pre AND during),
           averaged per parameter over its matched tables:
             DO mean(pre - during) >= 1.0 mg/l, or
             BOD mean(during - pre) >= 1.0 mg/l, or
             any parameter: paired mean pre within limit AND paired mean
             during breaching it (a "crossing").
           JUDGEMENT (1 mg/l is round and readable, not a CPCB number). Set
           after Malikhedi's DO pairs had been seen but before any other
           ghat's matched-pair results; not tuned after. Replaces festival >= 0.15.
           No matched pairs at all -> "Not enough data to tell".
  poor and not worse -> "Already poor before immersion"
  worse and not poor -> "Worsens with immersion"
  poor and worse     -> "Poor before and worse during"
  otherwise, Low tier         -> "Lower concern on available data"
  otherwise, Medium/High tier -> "Elevated on several measures; no single
                                 large change"
  The festival SCORE (used in risk) is unchanged; only the label uses the anchor.
  The output shows baseline, cut-off, festival score and anchor result next
  to the label (driver_detail). 0.40 is the Appendix A placeholder (not
  calibrated). The anchor was written after Malikhedi's DO pairs were seen.

NEAR-BOUNDARY: risk within 1.5 points of a tier line -> near_boundary = true,
  with the nearest line and the signed distance in the output.

UNPAIRED BREACHES: a during/after reading that breaches its limit but cannot
  be compared (no valid pre in its table; or an after reading with no valid
  during) is listed with value, parameter, festival-year, phase and source
  page. It does not change the score; it lowers confidence (below) and is
  shown in the evidence view. Never dropped silently.

CONFIDENCE:
  coverage = valid scored readings / expected scored readings
  expected = 6 festival-years (3 years x Ganesh/Durga) x 3 phases x 3 params
  High >= 0.90 | Medium 0.70-0.90 | Low < 0.70
  Also list the specific missing items (e.g. "no Durga 2025 table").
  If the latest Durga table is missing, cap confidence at Medium.
  If the site name match is "medium" (may be two places), cap at Medium and
  set site_match_uncertain with a note.
  If the ghat has any unpaired breach, lower confidence one level (floor Low).
  (JUDGEMENT; rules.yaml scoring.unpaired_breach_downgrade.)

RANKING: sort by tier, then risk score; break ties by lower confidence first
(the less certain ghat is listed higher for testing). Show score components.

REASON TEXT (generated by templates from numbers), example shape:
  "BOD breached its limit (at most 3 mg/l) in 3 of 4 pre-immersion readings;
   worst 6.8 mg/l (Durga 2024, Report 2024 p.26). Largest matched change
   during immersion: Dissolved oxygen 4.8 mg/l before to 3.8 mg/l during
   (Ganesh 2024, limit at least 5 mg/l; Report 2024 p.71). ..."
  Quote single readings or matched before/during pairs ONLY - never averages
  across different tables. Every number carries its report page. Unpaired
  breaches are named with their page and why they could not be compared.
  Never use words implying health outcomes.

APPENDIX F - ENHANCEMENT SPECS (defaults; tunable in rules.yaml)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F.1 PERSISTENCE
  For ghat g, parameter p in {DO, BOD, pH}, phase in {during, pre}:
    Y = number of festival-year tables with a VALID reading
    X = number of those readings that breach the class B limit
    M = number of tables where the reading is missing (shown, not counted)
  Label (during phase, per parameter):
    Y < 3                      -> "Too few tables"
    X >= ceil(2/3 * Y)         -> "Repeat"
    X == 1                     -> "One-off"
    otherwise                  -> "Intermittent"
  Display: "<X> of <Y> tables" and "<M> not sampled".
  Reason text example: "Dissolved oxygen was below 5 mg/l during immersion in
  5 of 6 tables."

F.2 TEST PLANNER
  uncertainty = 1 - coverage          (coverage from Appendix A)
  priority    = risk_score * (1 + u_weight * uncertainty)   u_weight = 1.0
  Order by priority desc; ties broken by lower coverage, then name.
  Take the first N ranked ghats.
  No-data designated ghats (from data/context/designated_ghats_2026.csv with
  zero valid readings) are listed separately as "No baseline"; with the
  checkbox ON they are shown first, wording: "No past readings: any test is
  new information." They get NO risk score and NO tier.
  What to test (text only): pre-immersion DO, BOD, pH before 20 Oct;
  during-immersion on the day. Say "data suggests testing", not "must".

F.3 SHARE-READY OUTPUT
  Text template slots: date, standard, top-3 ghats (name, tier, one number
  with year and page), one blind-spot line, one caveat line.
  <= 1,000 characters per language. Same numbers as the brief (single source:
  scoring outputs). Image: 1080x1080 PNG, same slots, high contrast, font
  size >= 28 px for body text.

F.4 STATEWIDE BENCHMARK (context only)
  Cleaning, in this order, logging rows removed at each step:
    1 drop rows where needs_check == "yes"
    2 normalise regional_office: strip spaces and dots, case-fold, merge
      known spelling variants (Singrouli/Singrauli, Chhindwada/Chhindwara)
    3 drop exact duplicates ignoring source_page
    4 drop rows whose site_as_printed matches any site_as_printed in the
      Bhopal CSV (no self-comparison)
    5 keep parameter in {pH, Dissolved oxygen, BOD}, phase == "during",
      status == value
  Site-level value = median of that site's during-phase readings (per
  parameter), so sites with many readings do not dominate.
  Percentile for ghat g, DO: share of peer sites with a HIGHER median than g
  (i.e. g is "lower than X% of peers"); BOD: share of peer sites with a LOWER
  median than g ("higher than X%"). pH is not benchmarked (range-based).
  Hide chip if peer sites < 30. Required wording on every chip: "context
  only; different water bodies; statewide file less checked".
  Isolation rule: benchmark code is read-only with respect to scores.
